# M6: advanced endless survival

> Moved verbatim from the old root `CLAUDE.md` (2026-11). Quoted section names (e.g. "Loot depth", "Run map") refer to other files in this folder (index: [README.md](README.md)). Test counts are the numbers recorded when the work landed, not current totals.

- **Survival director** (`survival/plan.ts`): `SurvivalPlanner(seed, tier)` decides per wave `{theme, event, boss, evolution, gates, budget}` sequentially from wave 1 (memoised), so plans depend on history (boss gaps, event cooldowns/repeat gaps, theme repetition) and are reproducible: seed = `(cfg.seed ?? hash(run id)) ^ 0x6d2b79f5` (`__rift.planFor(seed, tier, n)` mirrors it). Waves 1-3 stay hand-designed unless an event/boss is forced. `compose(n)` spends `waveBudget(n, tier)` (linear + mild quadratic x tier `budgetMult`) over the theme's kind mix using `ENEMY_COST`, with unlock waves (`ENEMY_UNLOCK`, pulled earlier by tier `unlockShift`), elite rolls (`eliteChance`, `maxModsFor`, elite cap), and a spawn cap (`spawnCap`, max 120, divided by the tier count multiplier): **surplus budget becomes per-spawn hpMult, never more entities**. Health/damage multipliers are the old `hpMultiplier`/`damageMultiplier` x tier (finite to wave 1e6; `safeWave`). `WaveManager` takes a `compose` callback (legacy `getWave` remains for old tests); `jumpTo`/`recompose` serve dev tools.
- **Bosses** are scheduled from `tier.bossFirstWave`, min gap `max(4, 7 - n/25)` with rising probability (forced after the window), never on evolution waves, alternating towards the less-met boss; repeat encounters get `bossScaling(k)` (log HP, faster cadence, extra summons, variants). Boss waves = boss + 45% escort budget. Warned in the prep phase (banner, alarm, HUD INCOMING chip).
- **Enemies**: `EnemyManager` is generic now: M0 kinds keep their inline logic; others register a `Behavior {create, spawn, update, damageMod, death, dying, release, hud}` (state in `e.ai`). `damage()` resolves behaviour modifiers (directional shields use `HitInfo.from`, phase immunity, boss weakpoints), then elite plating, for every source. `death()` may return seconds: the wreck leaves `active` (untargetable) and plays `dying()` before release (boss destruction sequences). `prebuild()` creates one of each rig at arena build; `prewarm()` (called each prep from the coming composition) builds pool objects one per frame.
  - Enforcer: frontal energy shield (x0.2 within ~70 deg) unless shocked/frozen/attacking; rear generator weakpoint; slams (telegraphed ring) towers/drone/reactor within reach. Artillery: stops at 30 m from the reactor, charges 2.1 s (laser + ground marker) then lobs a shell at the reactor rim or the nearest active tower (bias 55%); 12% max-HP damage or shock/freeze during the charge interrupts it; retreats along its own lane when the drone closes in. Engineer: holds 6 m behind the leading ally, heals (bosses at a tiny rate), restores shields, empowers (`buffT`: +30% dmg, +25% speed). Courier (HVT): orbits at 24 m / 6.8 m altitude, phase-shield windows, orbs, escapes after 45 s.
  - **Towers are suppressed, never destroyed** (`Turret.disable(s)`): artillery shells, slams, overcharged discharges, boss stomps/mortars/lances/sweeps, discharge LOS.
- **Elites** (`survival/elites.ts`): `validMods` enforces kinds + exclusions (armored x accelerated); `applyMods` keeps only compatible ones. Overcharged = telegraphed discharge (blue ring, cancelled by shock/freeze), Armored = plating absorbs 55% of non-weak, non-DoT damage until 35% max HP absorbed (plates fall off), Accelerated = x1.4 move, x1.5 attack rate, boosts, Volatile = 1.4 s telegraphed death blast (hurts drone/reactor/towers and other machines). Hardware per modifier (`eliteParts.ts`) + a threat mark (gold diamond with modifier pips; teal cross for support). Rewards: x(1 + 0.6/mod) cores/salvage/credits, the `elite` drop table (chance/luck per mod), component chance.
- **Bosses** (`enemies/bosses/*`): Colossus (4 legs, cannons, pods, lance, rear core doors): P1 volley (aim lines, lobbed shells at the drone's position), stomp (10 m ring, jump over it, suppresses towers), mortar bombs at towers; P2 (66%) ejects plates, missiles + summons, the rear core opens after attacks (x2.5 weakpoint); P3 (33%) siege beam at the reactor (3 s charge, interrupt with 6% max-HP damage) + combos. Overseer: emitters (`pylon` enemies orbiting it) make the core immune; killing them opens a 10 s exposure window, then redeploy; P2 (60%) relocation between combat positions, grid lances (towers/drone), prism sweep (marked line, core x2 while sweeping); P3 (28%) emitters burn out, overload discharge hits only targets in line of sight (take cover). Transitions: 1.7 s, damage x0.15 (never permanent). Bosses resist chill (`statusResist`; Bulwark stays at 1 to keep M5 freeze behaviour), take burn/shock normally, are targeted by towers.
- **Arena evolution** (`ArenaDynamics`): the south gate/lane exists from the start but is sealed (lane rendered grey; **its corridor is reserved for placement from the start**, as are the bulkhead footprints, `layout.reserved`). Evolution A (`routes`) slides the blast door open, removes its collider when open, lights the gate and adds `south` to plan gates. Evolution B (`instability`) raises two bulkheads (wait while the drone stands in the footprint, push it out after 4 s, collider only when fully raised) and arms four on-lane plasma vents (1.7 s warning ring, 0.9 s eruption: drone damage, burn on machines); towers within 7.5 m of a vent are conduit-fed (+25% fire rate). Windows: A at wave 7-10 (tier shifted), B 6-9 waves later, during prep.
- **Events** (`SurvivalRuntime`): one per wave max, never on boss waves, never consecutive, per-event repeat gap; rewards are collected into the run ledger at wave clear (banked at checkpoints like everything else). Swarm (x1.35 budget, 0.6 HP, faster), Elite Assault (squad of 4-6 two-mod elites through one gate + elite cache + components), Power Instability (telegraphed grid strikes every 3.6 s near the drone/machines/towers, tower overdrive +35% with short-out risk, surge credits per kill), HVT (Vault Courier; destroyed = signal-weighted `hvt` cache, escaped = no event bonus). `endEvent()` cleans strikes/markers and tower boosts at wave clear.
- **Hostile mechanics in Game**: `hurtPlayer` (all hostile damage), `hostileBlast` (drone falloff with cover check, reactor overlap, tower suppression, optional machine damage), `ordnanceImpact`, `ventTick`, `summon` (bounded at 46 active), `towerFireBoost`.
- **Shaders**: every kit template (all rigs incl. bosses and elite hardware) + telegraph/ordnance/machinery materials are force-compiled (`Game.m6Warmup`, awaited by the transition). Without this, unready materials are skipped for many frames in SwiftShader (bosses appeared without parts/glows).
- **Loot**: boss tables `colossus`/`overseer` (3 rolls, epic+) with exclusive bases (Colossus Siege Plating, Siege Capacitor module, Overseer Logic Core, Overseer Prism Lens module); new stat `eliteDamageMult` (applied in `Director.deal` to elites/bosses/HVT). Archetype tables (`enforcer` armor/shield, `artillery` weapon/processor/module, `engineer` reactor/accessory/module), `elite`, `eliteCache`, `hvt` (re-weighted to the announced signal category). Exclusive module bases are not craftable/buyable.
- **Tiers**: IV NIGHTMARE and V APEX added; every tier carries `budgetMult, eliteRate, maxMods, eventRate, bossFirstWave, bossPower, unlockShift, unlockNext {wave, boss}` (`unlocksNextTier`, `unlockText`). I->II and II->III at the wave-5 checkpoint (as before), III->IV at wave 15, IV->V at wave 20 with a boss destroyed in that run.
- **Records (save v6)**: `TierRecord` gains highestWave, bossesDefeated, elitesDefeated, bestBossesRun, bestPlayerDamage, bestTowerDamage, deaths, itemsBanked; totals gain boss/elite/death/damage sums and `devRuns`; `save.milestones`; history entries carry bosses/elites/deaths/damage/events/milestones/records/dev. `RunLedger.survival` (measured: kills, elites, bosses, deaths, reactor damage, `Director.dealt` player/tower damage copied by `Game.syncStats` before finalize) flows into `RunResult`. `recordRun` lists broken records (`result.records`). **Development sessions** (`run.survival.dev`, set by any M6 dev tool or `flow.devDeploy`) record a flagged history entry only; Flow's bank hook refuses them.
- **Milestones** (`survival/records.ts`) are evaluated from run statistics at wave clears and boss kills; the first time a profile reaches one (`ArenaConfig.milestones` snapshot), the run gets a cache (credits, components, `eliteCache` drop) that is unsecured until the next checkpoint. Tier achievements (wave 10 on IV/V) are milestones. Dev sessions get no rewards.
- **Dev tools**: F4 panel only with `?dev`/`?test` (`flow.devTools`); `__rift.dev.{jump,spawn,force,evolve,reset,deploy}`. F3 adds sim ms, AI ms, telegraphs, ordnance, wave/tier/event/boss, budget, spawn queue, elites, hp/dmg multipliers.
- **HUD**: boss bar (phase ticks, state, current telegraphed attack), INCOMING/EVENT/HVT chips, alert feed (max 3, de-duplicated, also first-sighting counterplay intel), elite/support/HVT name tags + bars within 45 m, radar colours per role.
- **Limitations / not done**: SwiftShader only (no real-GPU FPS); boss visuals are transform-animated kit geometry (no skinned meshes); the Colossus walks lane polylines and its legs can clip partition blocks at bends; enemies still never attack towers except by suppression; engineers can heal boss emitters; events and bosses do not stack; balance is first-pass.
